# Business website demos

- Hotel/index.html — The Palm House hotel demo.
- Hospital/index.html — existing hospital demo, renamed from Hospital-website.
- Boutique/index.html — Tara & Thread boutique demo.
- Gym/index.html — Mantra Fitness gym demo.

Open index.html to browse the collection. Each site is standalone with its own visitor sitemap, reviews and Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp links. Social destinations are demo placeholders; replace them before launch. No enquiry is sent or saved by these static demos.

## Initial client scope
Build basic informational business websites focused on service details, contacts, social links, location, visitor sitemaps and genuine approved reviews. All sites are informational only and do not collect bookings, appointments or payments. No staff dashboard or database is required for this scope. Customers contact the business directly.

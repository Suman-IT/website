# Mantra Fitness — informational website demo

Open index.html or serve the Website directory with python -m http.server 8000.

Files: index.html (content), styles.css (design), script.js (navigation), sitemap.html (visitor navigation).

This is a standalone, static website. No bookings, checkout, enquiry forms, data collection, database or staff dashboard. Contact buttons open the visitor's phone, email or WhatsApp app.

Before launch, replace the imaginary branding, service details, phone, email, address, opening hours, map destination and social links with verified client details. Reviews are explicitly labelled samples: replace with genuine approved reviews or remove the section. Confirm all services and membership inclusions with the client.

Layout and copy were created for this demo; they are not copied from an existing business website. Third-party assets: illustrative Unsplash photography and Google Fonts, loaded over the internet. Verify asset licensing for final use and prefer client-owned photography. Fallback fonts and background colours are included.

Create the search-engine XML sitemap and robots.txt after the final production domain and public URLs are known. The current sitemap.html is for visitors.
